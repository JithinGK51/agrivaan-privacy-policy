3. Android Permissions Required & Rationale

Our applications request specific device permissions to operate core functions. Below is an exhaustive list of all permissions declared in our Android Manifest files:

📍 Location Permissions (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION)

Used for: Detecting your current field location to search for available nearby machinery (Farmer App) and tracking equipment movement during active bookings (Owner App).

🔔 Notification Permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS & USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT)

Used for: Sending instant alerts regarding booking approvals, payment updates, driver dispatches, and lock-screen job notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM).

🔄 Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC)

Used for: Ensuring active trip synchronization and machinery status sync remains uninterrupted even when switching between apps during an active rental service.

📷 Camera & Storage Permissions (CAMERA, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE)

Used for: Allowing vehicle owners to photograph machinery and upload verification documents, and letting farmers upload profile pictures or field condition receipts.

💳 Direct Payment & Package Visibility Intents (PhonePe, GPay, Paytm, BHIM)

Used for: Enabling direct UPI Intent payment integration via Razorpay SDK, allowing seamless 1-click checkout through installed UPI payment applications on your device.

📌 Home Screen Widget (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED & APPWIDGET_UPDATE)

Used for: Updating glanceable home screen widgets on your phone for active booking statuses without requiring you to constantly open the app.

⚙️ How to Manage or Revoke Permissions You can grant or revoke any of these permissions at any time on your Android device by navigating to Settings > Apps & Notifications > Agrivaan Farmer App / Owner App > Permissions.

4. Third-Party SDKs & Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We integrate with trusted third-party service providers to power essential app functionality under strict data confidentiality agreements:

  • Google Maps Platform & Geolocation APIs: Renders interactive maps, calculates field-to-owner distances, and geocodes delivery addresses. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google Firebase (FCM & Analytics): Delivers secure real-time push notifications, manages app performance, and analyzes crash diagnostics. (Firebase Privacy Terms)
  • Razorpay Payment Gateway: Encrypts and processes online payments (UPI, Cards, Netbanking) and handles automated owner payouts. All financial transactions adhere to PCI-DSS standards. (Razorpay Privacy Policy)

5. Data Retention & Mandatory Account Deletion Policy

🗑️ Requesting Account & Data Deletion (Google Play Mandate)

Google Play Console policy mandates that all users must have a clear mechanism to request full account and data deletion. We fully honor your right to be forgotten.

Option A: In-App Account Deletion

  1. Open either the Agrivaan Farmer App or Agrivaan Owner App.
  2. Navigate to Account / Profile Settings.
  3. Tap on "Delete Account" and confirm your mobile number via OTP.

Option B: Web / Email Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the application or cannot access your phone, send an email to our Data Protection Team at support@agrivaan.com or crafzio@gmail.com with:

  • Subject: Account and Data Deletion Request
  • Your Registered Mobile Number
  • Application Used (Farmer App or Owner App)

What happens after your request:

All personal profile data, saved locations, vehicle listings, and FCM tokens will be permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days. Transactional records (such as tax invoices) will be retained solely to comply with applicable statutory tax laws.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our applications are designed exclusively for adult farmers, machinery owners, and agricultural service providers aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we discover that a minor has submitted personal information, we will delete it immediately.

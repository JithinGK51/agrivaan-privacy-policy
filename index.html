3. Android Permissions Required & Rationale

Our applications request only essential Android runtime permissions required for core agricultural functions:

📍 Location Permissions (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION)

Rationale: Enables the Farmer App to identify your exact field coordinates to match nearby tractors and implements, and allows the Owner App to compute transit routes and dispatch ETAs.

🔔 Notification Permissions & In-App Campaign Management (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

Rationale & Agricultural Necessity: Delivers time-critical push alerts for equipment booking confirmations, operator dispatch ETAs, hourly work timer OTPs, shop fertilizer delivery tracking, and emergency weather notices via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM).

Permission Tracking & Compliance Guarantees: To avoid attempting undeliverable push transmissions, our system verifies whether notifications are permitted at the operating system level (categorized as Granted, Denied, or Blocked). For users with disabled notifications, our app may periodically display a non-intrusive in-app prompt explaining the importance of dispatch alerts with a direct option to navigate to your device settings. We strictly comply with Google Play Store and Apple Developer Guidelines: we never attempt to bypass operating system controls, silently trigger notifications, or spam users. You retain full control to enable or disable notification permissions at any time via your device settings.

💳 Direct UPI App Package Visibility (queries tag)

Rationale: Enables seamless 1-click UPI checkout via the certified Razorpay SDK, allowing users to pay directly through installed UPI apps (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, BHIM).

📌 Home Screen Widget (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED & APPWIDGET_UPDATE)

Rationale: Updates real-time booking status on your phone's home screen widget without requiring continuous app launches.

🔄 Google Play In-App Update Management (Official Play Core API)

Rationale & Security: To guarantee uninterrupted service and protect farmers from security vulnerabilities, our mobile applications integrate Google Play's official In-App Update API (Play Core). When an update is detected, the app facilitates official Google Play installations via Flexible updates (background download with install prompt) or Immediate updates (critical full-screen upgrades for older builds below minimum supported versions). All update downloads are cryptographically verified and served exclusively by the Google Play Store; we never download APKs or binary files from unverified third-party hosts or sideloading mechanisms.

🛡️ Explicitly Removed Permissions (Privacy & Battery Protection)

Status: Permissions such as FOREGROUND_SERVICE and USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT have been explicitly removed (via manifest rules) to eliminate intrusive background operations and optimize device battery life.

⚙️ Managing Your App Permissions You can inspect, grant, or revoke permissions at any time through your Android settings: Settings > Apps > AgriVaan Farmer App / Owner App > Permissions.
